Pulse Kit

Four quick health calculators for everyday use. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere: every answer is worked out in your browser.

Body mass index

A quick screening number for adults (18+). It does not measure body fat or fitness.

Daily water intake

A general estimate: about 33 ml per kg of body weight, plus extra when you exercise.

Heart rate zones

Estimated maximum heart rate uses the Tanaka formula: 208 − 0.7 × age.

Sleep cycle planner

Sleep runs in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle can feel easier.

Not medical advice. These tools are for learning and general awareness only. They cannot diagnose or treat anything. If you feel unwell or have a health concern, talk to a doctor or a qualified health professional.
